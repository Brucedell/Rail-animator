Vehicles and pivots

What a “vehicle” is in data terms. It is not a list of N identical pivots but a few distinct roles, together with how the body gets its position and angle, and how a rig plugs into the mechanism.

6.2 Pivot roles

The v8 pivot list treats every pivot the same (“offset in metres, own Follow Path”). That is correct for bogies but not enough for locomotives. The vehicle-architecture session already identified three roles, and adding a fourth covers everything:

RoleMechanism objectHow its transform is found
TRACKEDEmpty + Follow Path (as today)Route point at d + offset, with that point's heading and tilt.
AXLE_POINTEmpty + Follow PathSame as TRACKED. Only its position is used; the frame's heading comes from the pair.
RADIALEmpty + Follow Path (axle) + stored pin_offsetAxle on rail; the truck bone sits at the axle and Damped Tracks toward the pin on the frame.
RIGID_MOUNTNone (bone in the rig)Child of the frame bone.

6.3 Where the root goes

Option A: root at the vehicle's centre

The root sits midway between the two defining points (bogie centres, or the ends of the rigid wheelbase). Pivot offsets are symmetric (±). It looks natural in the viewport and matches how most models are built. Coupler positions are stored as front_coupler / rear_coupler distances in the definition.

Option B: root at the front coupler

Consist maths becomes one running number, but every pivot offset is negative and the gizmo sits at the nose. It is awkward to author, and flipping the vehicle's facing moves the reference point.

Recommendation

Option A (centre), plus coupler distances in the definition. The consist code computes spacing as rear_coupler(car n) + gap + front_coupler(car n+1), and the user only enters the gap (often 0 when couplers touch). That keeps the “front-referenced chain” idea from session 4 without making the vehicle awkward to author.

6.4 Body orientation and the chord effect

A long car on a curve does not hug the rail: its body is a straight line between its two bogies, so its middle sits inside the curve and its ends overhang outside. Today's root rides the rail itself, so a mesh parented straight to the root clips through the inside of tight curves.

Built with native constraints on the body bone

BODY bone
  Copy Location   → Pivot_Front Empty           influence 1.0
  Copy Location   → Pivot_Rear  Empty           influence 0.5   (= midpoint of the two)
  Damped Track    → Pivot_Front Empty  (Y)      heading along the chord
  Copy Rotation   → Pivot_Front (roll only, mix ADD, influence 0.5)
  Copy Rotation   → Pivot_Rear  (roll only, mix ADD, influence 0.5)   averaged banking

For a steam loco the same pattern uses the two driver AXLE_POINTs instead of bogies (Part 10).

6.7 Facing and running backwards

A locomotive can run tender-first, and a coach can be turned end for end. Add a per-vehicle facing (+1 / −1):

  • Pivot offsets and coupler ends are multiplied by facing when placing points.
  • Follow Path on the root and pivots uses forward_axis = 'FORWARD_Y' when facing = +1 and 'TRACK_NEGATIVE_Y' when −1, so the model turns 180° with no rig change.
  • Wheel rotation sign flips automatically, because it is computed from distance × facing.
  • This is the “direction flipping system” saved for later in session 5. It is a small addition once pivots are role-based.

6.8 The Vehicle Definition

Everything above that describes the model (not its current position) belongs in a definition saved with the asset:

  front_coupler: +5.90 m    rear_coupler: -5.90 m      facing_default: +1
  pivots:
    LEAD_TRUCK   role RADIAL      offset +3.95  pin_offset +2.60  bone TRUCK_F
    DRIVER_1     role AXLE_POINT  offset +1.05                    bone AXLE_D1
    DRIVER_2     role AXLE_POINT  offset -1.05                    bone AXLE_D2
    TRAIL_TRUCK  role RADIAL      offset -3.95  pin_offset -2.60  bone TRUCK_R
  frame: aims DRIVER_2 → DRIVER_1, bone FRAME
  wheel_groups:
    drivers   bones [WHL_D1_L, WHL_D1_R, WHL_D2_L, WHL_D2_R]  diameter 1.73 m  mode AUTO
    trucks    bones [WHL_F_L, WHL_F_R, WHL_T_L, WHL_T_R]      diameter 0.97 m  mode AUTO
